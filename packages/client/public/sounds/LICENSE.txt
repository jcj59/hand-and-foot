The card sounds in this directory are from "Casino Audio" (1.1) by Kenney
(https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), released under Creative Commons Zero
(CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Converted to mono
22.05 kHz WAV so every browser plays them.

  draw.wav       <- card-slide-1.ogg
  discard.wav    <- card-place-1.ogg
  meld.wav       <- card-place-2.ogg
  take-back.wav  <- card-slide-2.ogg

  (Taking the pile plays draw.wav lower and slower.)

The emote sounds (emote-*.mp3) are from Mixkit (https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/),
under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License (https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree). Trimmed
of silence, cut to at most 1.8 seconds with a short fade, evened out to -16 LUFS and
converted to mono 44.1 kHz MP3.

  emote-laugh.mp3      <- #2882 "Cartoon laugh voice"
  emote-cry.mp3        <- #474  "Lost kid sobbing"
  emote-angry.mp3      <- #1968 "Small monster growl"
  emote-thumbs-up.mp3  <- #2358 "Long pop"
  emote-wow.mp3        <- #967  "Cartoon surprise gasp"
  emote-party.mp3      <- #530  "Happy party horn sound"
  emote-grimace.mp3    <- #395  "Cartoon falling whistle"
  emote-cool.mp3       <- #3108 "Crystal chime"
